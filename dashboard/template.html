What the checks look for

Every invoice went through 4 checks. Each one catches a different way a bill can be wrong.

  • Rate above contractCharged a higher price per hour / unit than the contract says.
  • Quantity above usageBilled for more hours or units than our records show we used.
  • Unexplained feeThe total is more than price × quantity — an extra charge with no reason.
  • Likely duplicateThe same bill sent twice within 14 days, with no new work behind it.
